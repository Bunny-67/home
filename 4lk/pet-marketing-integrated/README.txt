INTEGRATED VERSION

This package is arranged like your existing website so 4leggedkids.html will immediately load the pet-page styles/interactions when placed at the root of your site.

Included:
- 4leggedkids.html
- assets/css/style.css  (your original style.css + pet page styles appended)
- assets/css/nav.css    (your original nav.css unchanged)
- assets/js/java.js     (your original java.js + pet page interactions appended)

To use on your real site:
1. Put 4leggedkids.html beside index.html/about.html/projects.html.
2. Replace your current assets/css/style.css with this integrated copy OR paste only the appended pet section at the bottom of your existing file.
3. Keep nav.css as-is.
4. Replace assets/js/java.js with this integrated copy OR paste the pet interaction section at the bottom of your existing script.
5. Add photos later by replacing the placeholder blocks in 4leggedkids.html with image tags.

The styling is scoped to body.pet-pitch-page, so it should not alter your other pages.
