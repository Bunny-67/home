Client Spotlight: Harmony Chiropractic

For Harmony Chiropractic in South Elgin, IL, I produced a full suite of testimonial and promotional content for use on Facebook and Instagram. I handled directing, filming, lighting, sound design, editing, and graphics across a campaign of short-form videos.

Over a six-month engagement, I delivered multiple cuts ranging from 30 seconds to 2 minutes, designed to feel friendly, trustworthy, and tailored to their patient community.

Software: Adobe Premiere, Photoshop, and Audition.
